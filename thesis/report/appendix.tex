\section{Hyperparameter Tables and Configurations}

\Cref{tab:training-hyperparams} lists the hyperparameters used to train the bidirectional model of \Cref{sec:training-procedure}. The tuned values (learning rate, weight decay, batch size, latent dimension, dropout, normalisation, the spectrum-loss and cycle-consistency weights, and the curriculum schedule) are the optimum selected by an Optuna hyperparameter search ($20$ trials); the remaining entries (network widths, epochs, spectrum binning) were fixed a priori.
